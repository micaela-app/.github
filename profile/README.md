  # Lebenix                                                                                                              
                                                           
  > B2B SaaS platform for nutritionists in LATAM — patient management, appointments, clinical measurements and meal      
  plans.                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                         
  ## What is Lebenix?                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                         
  Lebenix helps nutritional centers in Latin America manage their practice from a single platform:                       
                                                                                                                         
  - **Patient management** — records, clinical history, tags and measurements
  - **Appointments** — calendar, scheduling and confirmations
  - **Meal plans** — personalized nutrition planning with templates, recipes and food database
  - **Team** — multi-role access (admin, professional, staff) with granular permissions
  - **Google Calendar** — two-way sync for appointments
  - **White label** — custom domain and brand colors (Business plan)
  - **Multi-location** — manage multiple offices under one account
